## Assets

Nothing in this repository was imported from another project. There are no
image, audio, font or data files from outside sources.

|---|---|---|

## Facts that belong to nobody

Blackjack rules, basic strategy, the Hi-Lo count and the index numbers known
as the Illustrious 18 are mathematics and common knowledge of the game. Only
this project's own wording and presentation of them is licensed here.

## Deliberately left out

The cartridge header's boot-logo field is zeroed. That data is Nintendo's
trademark and is not redistributed (`src/crt0.s`).
